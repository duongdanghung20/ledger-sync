# Book as the accounting-entity boundary

An Organization owns one or more Books, and a Book — not the Organization — is the boundary for all financial data: each Book maps to exactly one QuickBooks company (`realmID`) and owns its own Chart of Accounts, Bank Accounts, Imported Transactions, Categorization Rules, and Journal Entries. We introduced Book as a first-class object, rather than scoping financial data directly to the Organization, because a single business can hold multiple legal entities / QuickBooks companies with different charts of accounts, and QuickBooks itself is company-scoped via `realmID`. v1 ships exactly one Book per Organization, auto-created at setup, with multi-Book creation and switching deferred; the schema carries Book from the start so a second entity needs no migration of the money tables. Roles (Admin, Bookkeeper) remain Organization-wide in v1; per-Book role scoping is deferred.
